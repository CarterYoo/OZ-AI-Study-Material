## Data

The interactive cost-function lab uses **Auto MPG**, R. Quinlan (1993), UCI Machine Learning Repository, [DOI: 10.24432/C5859H](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5859H), under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The included CSV is the [seaborn-data copy](https://github.com/mwaskom/seaborn-data/blob/master/mpg.csv). The lab sorts by weight and selects eight observations for calculation practice, rather than for evaluating predictive performance.

Other worksheet datasets are synthetic teaching examples. They should not be presented as measurements from a real study.
